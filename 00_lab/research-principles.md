# Research Principles

1. **Never assume a Gap exists without evidence** from the literature.
2. Always separate: what the paper says / what the data proves / our inference / our hypothesis.
3. We reject questions that require unavailable proprietary data, unless a realistic alternative exists.
4. We prefer reproducible experiments.
5. Every experiment must specify up-front: input data, Baseline, metric, expected outcome, hardware and software requirements.
6. A Prototype is not a Contribution on its own.
7. We challenge Confirmation Bias — the Critical Reviewer is mandatory after every result.
8. Any claim that needs independent verification is recorded in the decision log.
